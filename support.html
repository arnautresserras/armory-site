Privacy policy

Support

Forever Armory looks up World of Warcraft: Forever characters on your iPhone: their gear, stats, talents, skills and reputation, with progress and a gear plan for the ones you pin. Questions or bug reports? Email arnautresserras@gmail.com. Replies usually come within a few days.

Is this an official Blizzard app?

No. Forever Armory is an unofficial fan app, made by a fan. It is not affiliated with, endorsed or sponsored by Blizzard Entertainment.

My character isn't found

  • Check the spelling of both the first name and the surname.
  • Try the other region. The app covers the US and EU regions, and the same name can exist in both.
  • New characters appear in Blizzard's armory only after they reach level 10 and log out.

The portrait isn't there yet

Blizzard draws a character's portrait some hours after it logs out. Until then the app shows a silhouette. Gear and the other tabs work as normal.

The gear or talents are out of date

Blizzard updates a character after it logs out, and the app keeps each answer for about an hour. Log out in game, wait a little, then look the character up again.

Progress is empty

Progress is recorded for pinned characters only, at most once a day: when you open the app or view the character. On the day you pin it there is nothing to compare yet. Open the app on later days and the history fills in.

What does "Open in Reroll calculator" do?

It opens the character's talents in Reroll Forever, a free companion app for planning talents. If Reroll isn't installed, it opens the same build on Reroll's website.

Does the app need an account or internet?

No account. Looking up a character needs an internet connection. Pins and recent searches stay on your phone; see the privacy policy.

How do I get a refund for a tip?

Tips are in-app purchases handled by Apple, so refunds go through Apple too: request one at reportaproblem.apple.com.
