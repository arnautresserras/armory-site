Look up any Forever character

Gear, stats and talents as the game shows them, plus progress and a gear plan for every character you pin. Free on iPhone.

Download on the App Store

The character screen, as in the game

Skills and reputation get a tab each too, with every faction’s standing.

  1. Tap a slot for the tooltip

    Stats, enchant, durability, set bonuses and sell price, in the game’s own colours.

  2. Talents, then Reroll

    Every tree with its points and picks. One tap opens the build in the Reroll Forever calculator.

  3. A card for the guild chat

    Share the portrait, item level and region as an image, to any chat.

Follow your pins, plan their gear

New in 1.1. Every character you pin gets a daily history and a gear planner, kept on your iPhone.

  1. Progress, day by day

    Levelling pace, item level, gear changes and skill-ups while you level. At 60, the key stats for your spec, with their caps.

  2. A plan for every slot

    Pick a target item for each slot on the paper doll and see the plan against what you wear. Targets tick off once you equip them.

  3. Upgrades for your spec

    Rank items for every slot by one of your spec’s key stats, filter by quality and level, and set the best as targets.
